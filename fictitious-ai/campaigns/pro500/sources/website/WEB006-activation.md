# Make activation a definition you can defend

An activation metric should express a meaningful product behavior. If the definition changes whenever a chart is inconvenient, the metric stops helping the team decide.

## Write the definition before the conclusion

State the event or sequence you are using, the eligible population, and the period you are examining. Keep the distinction between a hypothesis about value and evidence of a completed behavior.

## Compare the surrounding behavior

Use Fictitious AI to examine events, funnels, and cohorts around your proposed activation point. Ask a natural-language question when useful, then review its interpretation against the definition you wrote.
