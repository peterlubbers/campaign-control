# Questions about Fictitious AI

## What does Fictitious AI help us examine?

Product behavior through events, funnels, retention, and cohorts. You can also ask natural-language questions and review their interpretation before relying on an analysis.

Pro500 includes sharing for up to five teammates.

## Does an answer replace our review?

No. Review the question, definitions, relevant population, and interpretation. A generated answer is not a guarantee that the underlying business conclusion is correct.

## What should we bring to a walkthrough?

Bring a question you would like to investigate, your current definition of the relevant behavior, and the decision you hope the evidence will inform. Avoid including personal or sensitive data in an initial inquiry.

## What if a requirement is not described here?

Ask us to clarify it. Do not assume a feature or commercial term from silence.
