# From an event to a better question

Fictitious AI is a product analytics workspace for examining how people use a product.

## Events describe what happened

Begin with a defined action. Before interpreting a count, establish what the event means and which people or sessions the analysis includes.

## Funnels describe a journey

Choose the steps that represent an intended outcome. Review the sequence and the population before treating a change between steps as a product problem.

## Retention and cohorts add context

Define the return behavior that matters, then compare groups deliberately. A cohort is useful when its membership answers the question you are asking.

## Natural language still needs review
