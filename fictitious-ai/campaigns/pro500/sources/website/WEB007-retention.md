# Ask what returning really means

Opening a product again and completing a valuable task again answer different questions. Choose the return behavior that matches the decision you need to make.

## Define the beginning and the return

A retention investigation needs a starting behavior, a returning behavior, and a time window. Record all three before comparing cohorts. Different definitions may produce different pictures without either chart being wrong.

## Keep interpretation separate from observation

Fictitious AI helps you examine retention and cohorts. A pattern can guide a follow-up question, but it does not establish a cause or guarantee what a product change will do.
