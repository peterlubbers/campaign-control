# Separate the evidence from the business bet

An analysis can show a pattern without establishing what caused it or what will happen next. Keep that distinction visible when deciding how to use the result.

For your Fictitious AI evaluation, write three short statements: the behavior you observed, the interpretation you want to test, and the decision that depends on it. Review whether each statement has the support it needs.
