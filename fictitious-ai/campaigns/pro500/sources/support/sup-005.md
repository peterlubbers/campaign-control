# Check the groups in a cohort comparison

## State why the groups matter

A useful cohort comparison begins with a reason for comparing those users. Describe the group definitions and what you hope to learn.

## Inspect possible differences

Use Fictitious AI to explore cohorts, then ask whether the populations and time windows support the interpretation. A difference in behavior alone does not prove a cause.
