# Review a natural-language answer

## Read the question and the answer together

Confirm that the answer addresses the question you intended to ask. Check whether an event, population, or time window was interpreted differently.

## Inspect the reasoning

Fictitious AI supports natural-language analysis of product behavior. A clear explanation can still depend on an assumption that needs review.

## Ask a better follow-up

Name the uncertainty directly and narrow the next question. Preserve the distinction between observed behavior and a proposed explanation.
