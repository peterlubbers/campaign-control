# Read a funnel without assuming the cause

## Define the sequence

Name the steps in the journey and explain why they belong in this order. Check the users and observation window included in the question.

## Inspect the transition

Fictitious AI helps you explore funnels and ask natural-language questions about product behavior. Identify the transition that deserves a closer look.

## Keep the interpretation bounded

A pattern in the funnel does not prove why it happened. Record a plausible question for further investigation instead of presenting it as a confirmed cause.
