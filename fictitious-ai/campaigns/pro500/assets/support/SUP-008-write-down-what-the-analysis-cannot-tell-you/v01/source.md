# Write down what the analysis cannot tell you

## Separate observation from explanation

A change in behavior may be visible before its cause is understood. Record what you observed and label any proposed explanation as an interpretation.

## Name the missing evidence

Write the question that would help you distinguish between explanations. State which definition or observation would make the next investigation useful.

## Invite a different reading

A thoughtful team can challenge a conclusion without dismissing the work. Ask another reader to identify the assumption they would check first.

## Leave an honest ending

A useful analysis can finish with an unresolved question. Clear limits make the next decision more informed.
