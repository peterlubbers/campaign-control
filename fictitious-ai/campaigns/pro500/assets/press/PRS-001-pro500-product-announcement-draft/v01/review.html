Fictitious AI introduces Pro500 for product analytics

Fictitious AI is a fictional AI-assisted product analytics company. Its Pro500 offer brings event, funnel, retention and cohort investigation into a workflow where people review the assumptions behind an answer.

The original offer is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates. Product managers and analysts can use the shared workspace to examine a question and discuss what the available events do and do not establish.

Natural-language questions support investigation; they do not remove the need for human judgment. This announcement makes no performance, customer-count or business-outcome claim.
