# Evaluate a real onboarding journey

Use a walkthrough to examine a question from your product, rather than a collection of unrelated charts.

Start with the first task a new user is trying to complete. Identify the behaviors that would show meaningful progress. Then ask where a funnel would be useful and where a different view is needed.

During the review, keep a separate list of observations and hypotheses. A change between funnel steps can help you decide what to investigate; it does not establish the cause of the behavior.
