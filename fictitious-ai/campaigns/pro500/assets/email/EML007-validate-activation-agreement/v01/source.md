# Can everyone explain the activation definition?

Before adopting an activation metric, ask the people reviewing it to explain the definition in their own words.

Do they name the same behavior? Do they use the same eligible population? Do they distinguish an observed action from a hypothesis about value? If the answers differ, resolve the definition before building a decision around the chart.

You can use this exercise during a Fictitious AI walkthrough. Examine the events and relevant funnel, then compare the result with the question you meant to ask. If a natural-language answer is involved, review how it interpreted that question.

Bring one disagreement to the conversation. A useful evaluation makes the disagreement concrete enough to address.

Read the activation guide and note the decision your definition needs to support.
