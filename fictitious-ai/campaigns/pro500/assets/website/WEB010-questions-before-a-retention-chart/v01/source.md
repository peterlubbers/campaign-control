# Three questions to ask before a retention chart

## What behavior starts the relationship?

Choose a starting event that matches the question. A signup, a first completed task, and a first purchase describe different beginnings. Write down why the selected event is useful.

## What counts as returning?

Decide whether a return means any activity or a particular meaningful action. Neither choice is universally correct. The definition needs to fit the product decision under discussion.

## Which comparison is fair?

A cohort needs enough time to exhibit the behavior being measured. When comparing groups, examine whether their observation windows and definitions are comparable.

Use these questions to frame an investigation before interpreting the result. Treat the chart as evidence within that investigation, and keep hypotheses about causes separate from the behaviors you actually observed.
