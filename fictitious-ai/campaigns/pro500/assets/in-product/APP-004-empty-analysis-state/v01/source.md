# A good analysis starts with a specific question

This space is ready for a product question. Try comparing the steps between account creation and a meaningful first action, or examining whether a clearly defined cohort returns over time.

Before you begin, choose the event definitions and a time window. Keep a note of what the events can tell you and what they leave out.
