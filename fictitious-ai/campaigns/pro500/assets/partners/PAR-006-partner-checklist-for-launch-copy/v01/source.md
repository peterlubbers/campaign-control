# Partner checklist for launch copy

## Check the product description

Describe Fictitious AI as a fictional AI product analytics SaaS supporting events, funnels, retention, cohorts, and natural-language analysis.

## Check the offer

Use Pro500, $500/month, and sharing for up to five teammates. Review headlines, body copy, captions, and metadata together so they describe the same offer.

## Check the implications

Do not turn a product demonstration into a promise of revenue, conversion, accuracy, or retention improvement. Avoid unstated product capabilities.

## Check the handoff

Confirm that a human has reviewed the material and chosen a destination before distribution. This checklist does not authorize publishing.
