# Joint discussion: better product questions

## Discussion purpose

Explore what makes a product analytics question useful before choosing a chart or drawing a conclusion.

## Conversation outline

Start with an event definition. Discuss where a funnel invites investigation, what retention means in a particular context, and why a cohort comparison needs a reason.

## Product example

Use Fictitious AI to illustrate the workflow with fictional material. Pro500 is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates.

## Closing exercise

Ask the audience to name one assumption they would verify before acting on an analysis. Do not present a demonstration as measured business impact.
