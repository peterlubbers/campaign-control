# Webinar: one product question, a reviewable answer

## Opening

Welcome participants to a fictional Fictitious AI product walkthrough. Ask them to choose an activation question they would want to investigate; use invented events throughout the demonstration.

## Demonstration sequence

Define account creation and the first meaningful action. Examine the funnel, name the cohort, and inspect the assumptions in the AI-assisted explanation. Show how an observation can produce a follow-up question without establishing causation.

## Commercial checkpoint

Explain that Pro500 is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates. Demonstrate a shared review only within that original allowance. Do not invent event limits, discounts or transition policies.

## Closing

Ask participants which assumption they would verify before using the result. Offer an evaluation conversation centered on their question and the confirmed product facts.
