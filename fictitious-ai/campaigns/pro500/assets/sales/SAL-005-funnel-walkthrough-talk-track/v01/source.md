# Funnel walkthrough talk track

## Frame the investigation

Begin with one transition in a product journey. Ask what the sequence means and why this transition deserves attention.

## Inspect the analysis

Use Fictitious AI to explore the funnel. Check event definitions, the selected population, and the observation window before discussing the result.

## Avoid the causal leap

A drop-off identifies a question to investigate. It does not establish the reason users stopped, nor does it prove a proposed change will improve conversion.
