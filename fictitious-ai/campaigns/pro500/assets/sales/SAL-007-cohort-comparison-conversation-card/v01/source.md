# Cohort comparison conversation card

## Name the comparison

Explain why the selected groups belong in the same question. Note whether the groups differ in ways that could affect interpretation.

## Explore the pattern

Fictitious AI supports cohort analysis and natural-language questions. Ask what the comparison shows, which definitions it uses, and what it leaves unresolved.

## Keep the conclusion bounded

A difference between cohorts is an observation. It is not, by itself, proof of a cause or a recommendation to change the product.

Pro500 is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates. Use a fictional or permitted example to evaluate how reviewers discuss the same comparison.
