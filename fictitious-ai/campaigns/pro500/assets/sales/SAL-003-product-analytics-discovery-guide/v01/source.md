# Product analytics discovery guide

## Ask about the decision

Which product decision is waiting on a clearer understanding of behavior? Ask for a specific example rather than a general wish for more dashboards.

## Clarify the measurement

What event represents the action? Which users and time window matter? Who checks the definition before the result is shared?

## Connect the workflow

Fictitious AI supports exploration of events, funnels, retention, cohorts, and natural-language questions. Pro500 is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates.

## Close the discovery

Choose a bounded evaluation and record what would count as useful evidence. Do not infer a performance guarantee, integration, or commercial term that has not been established.
