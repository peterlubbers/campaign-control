# Product analytics evaluation scorecard

## Define a fair comparison

Choose one question that can be answered with fictional or permitted product data. Record the current workflow and the review it requires.

## Measure the full task

Track time spent framing the question, checking definitions, exploring the analysis, correcting misunderstandings, and reviewing the conclusion. A fast generated answer is only one part of the task.

## Evaluate Fictitious AI

Pro500 is $500/month with sharing for up to five teammates. Compare the reviewed outcome and open questions with the original process.

## Make the result explicit

Report observed effort and limitations. Do not turn a single demonstration into an unmeasured revenue or conversion claim.
